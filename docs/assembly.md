Ok so How to assemble?

1. print out all the 3D printed parts and get all the components ready.
2. Attach the 2 extrusion peices together to get 1 longer extrusion.
3. Then glue the gliding parts 3'd printed parts to the gliding carrige.
4. then put the carriage through the extrusion in the center of it. 
5. Then on 1 side attach the 3D printed servo mount to the extrusion, and attach the servo to the mount.
6. pass the threaded rod through the carriages nut and connect it to the adapter to the servo
7. on the other end of the extrusion attach the syringe holder.
8. insert a syringe (dont forget to remove the needle!)

Thats the mechanical part done, for electronic now connect the servo to its driver and connect driver to arduino as shown in wiring diagram, then connect the LCd with the i2c and then finally the potentiometer, load the code onto the arduino and start pumping!
